Our focus

How We Support Child Education

School Fees Sponsorship

Covering tuition for disadvantaged children through our Scholarship Scheme so no child is turned away.

School Supplies

Distributing uniforms, sandals, bags, and learning materials so every child is ready to learn.

Mentorship Programs

Connecting children with mentors who inspire, guide, and encourage them toward a brighter future.

In the field

Moments from the outreach

Backpack handovers, uniform drives, and Children's Day celebrations from communities across Nigeria.

Beyond the classroom

Scholarships, Debates & Relief Support

Education support goes further than supplies — from scholarship cheques to interschool debates and relief items for the whole family.

Scholarship Fund

Direct scholarship awards presented to standout pupils to keep them in school.

Debate & Quiz Competitions

Our Children's Day interschool debate and quiz competitions build confidence, critical thinking, and give a platform to talk about issues like bullying.

Family Relief Packs

Because a hungry child struggles to learn, we pair school outreaches with food and household relief items for families.
