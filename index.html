Welcome to BBSI

Where compassion meets action

Blue Baby Support Initiative is committed to standing in the gap for individuals and families facing difficult circumstances, particularly women and children. We believe that support should go beyond sympathy. Sometimes, what people need is an opportunity, access to essential resources, someone willing to listen, or simply someone willing to step in when help is needed.

Through education, maternal and family support, empowerment, advocacy and community outreach, we work alongside individuals and communities to create meaningful and lasting impact.

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn. Every woman deserves support. Every family deserves hope.

The conversations we refuse to ignore

BBSI is committed to raising awareness and encouraging meaningful conversations around issues that affect women, children and families. We lend our voice to conversations around maternal health, childbirth complications, newborn wellbeing, mental health after childbirth, access to education and the wider challenges faced by vulnerable communities. Awareness is not just about talking about a problem; it is about helping people understand it, respond to it and work towards change

From our founder

The story behind BBSI

When a personal experience becomes a purpose

Growing up, I watched my parents open our home and their hearts to people in need. Helping others was not something we talked about; it was simply something I saw and experienced.

Years later, life gave me a personal understanding of how quickly circumstances can change.

During the birth of my daughter, I experienced serious complications, while my newborn required urgent medical care. What should have been one of the happiest moments of my life became a period of fear, uncertainty and helplessness.

We were fortunate to receive the care and support we needed. But that experience left me with a question I could not ignore: What happens to the mothers and children who do not get that second chance?

That question became part of my purpose.

Blue Baby Support Initiative was born from a desire to turn a personal experience into something that could bring hope, practical support and opportunities to others.

In every difficult moment, I believe there can be purpose. BBSI is my commitment to making sure that, wherever possible, no woman, child or family must face life's challenges alone."

— Aisha Dania Ogieriakhi, Founder, BBSI
