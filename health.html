Maternal Health Support

We work to reduce preventable maternal and infant mortality by providing health education, facilitating access to prenatal and postnatal care, and supporting women through gestation with essential supplies and resources.

Our focus

How we support maternal health

Health Education

Teaching mothers and families about safe pregnancy, delivery, and newborn care practices.

Prenatal & Postnatal Care

Facilitating access to antenatal check-ups, safe delivery support, and postpartum follow-up care.

Essential Supplies

Providing medical supplies, hygiene kits, and resources women need throughout gestation.
