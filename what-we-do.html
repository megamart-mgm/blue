What We Do

Creating Lasting Change for Women and Children

At Blue Baby Support Initiative (BBSI), we are committed to creating lasting change by addressing the critical needs of women, children, and families in underserved communities across Nigeria. Our programs are designed to provide immediate relief while fostering long-term empowerment and sustainability.

Our core programs

How we create lasting change

Maternal and Infant Health Advocacy
We work tirelessly to reduce preventable maternal and infant mortality rates by providing health education, facilitating access to prenatal and postnatal care, and supporting women during gestation with essential supplies and resources.
Emergency Relief for Single Mothers
We provide contingency funds, food, and essential relief to single mothers and families in crisis, standing in the gap as a safety net when they need it most.
Educational Support for Vulnerable Children
Through our Back-to-School project and Scholarship Scheme, we provide school fees, uniforms, sandals, and learning materials so that more children have the opportunity to succeed in school.
Rural Empowerment Programs
We equip rural women with income-generating skills, training, and opportunities to build sustainable livelihoods, reduce poverty, and achieve economic independence.
Community Outreach and Engagement
We hold medical outreaches, organize festive events, and regularly feed and empower vulnerable families across communities including Benue, Ebutte Metta, Ikorodu, Abeokuta, and Ibadan.
Advocacy for Social Change
We partner with local initiatives and stakeholders to promote maternal and child health, and to combat harmful cultural practices that put women and children at risk.
Our impact areas

Through our comprehensive programs, we focus on:

  • Reducing maternal and infant mortality rates.
  • Providing a safety net for single mothers in crisis.
  • Ensuring access to quality education for vulnerable children.
  • Empowering women to achieve economic independence.
  • Strengthening communities through targeted interventions.
